Why SJMC?

No matter what major a student chooses, their time spent at the School of Journalism and Mass Communication will be an unforgettable ride. From their beginnings in the trenches of J202, to their respective capstones, every SJMC student will make meaningful personal and professional connections, amazing memories and impressive portfolio content that will set them up for success in the future. The amazing faculty, alumni connections and fellow students that the SJMC has to offer allow students to comfortably find their footing within their practice with confidence

Journalism

Under the journalism major, students learn the principles and practices of reporting, master writing and editing in AP style, training their skills in writing succinct and impactful news stories, while also having the opportunity to explore multimedia journalism such as filming, audio editing, graphic designing, and social media campaigning

Details

Strategic Communications

Under the strategic communications major, students learn the basics of advertising and strategic communication, how to cater messages to particular audiences, how to successfully produce and execute an advertising campaign, while also practicing social media advertising skills and adjusting to the ever-changing industry of communication
